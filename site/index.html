It sees what you see

Dumbledore reads your screen all the time while it runs: every page, document and AI chat in front of you. That’s how it knows what you’re studying without you telling it, notices when something doesn’t click, and builds your notes and gaps on its own. Pause it any time from the menu bar, and exclude apps or sites you never want it to see.

Install in two minutes

  1. Download Dumbledore, open the file and drag Dumbledore to Applications.
  2. Open Dumbledore. Because it isn’t notarized by Apple yet, macOS stops it the first time. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  3. Allow Accessibility and Screen Recording when Dumbledore asks. This is what lets it watch your screen and hear the right ⌥ key.
  4. Tell Dumbledore what you’re studying. That’s it: open your AI chat and tap right ⌥.

Dumbledore works right away with a free AI service, which can be slow. For fast answers, add your own Gemini key in Settings → AI providers. What it sees becomes notes stored on your Mac; text it needs to think about is sent to the AI provider. It never reads password managers or apps you exclude.
